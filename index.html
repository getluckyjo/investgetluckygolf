  • Signed by Golfzon, checked by the insurer. A forged ace fails.

07 · The risks

What could go wrong, and what we do about it.

Will Golfzon's bays carry this, and what if Korea says no?

Phase one runs on features Golfzon's bays already have, and the integration is built. Most rounds are in Korea, where paid-entry prizes still need clearing; the plan assumes 2027. On the rest of the network alone the round marks at 2.17×. Without Korea and without Virgin Active it's 1.12×, and cash would run to Q2 2028; we'd know about Korea during 2027 and cut costs well before then.

Will an insurer cover a simulator ace?

Santam has agreed in principle. After commission the cover breaks even at one ace in 5,208; Golfzon measures about one in 6,992. That's why we play the hardest setting, rotate the hole weekly and cap entries. If the premium lands at 30%, not 24%, it comes out of our share and the round marks at 3.12×. Indwe both sponsors us and places the cover, so a second carrier joins the panel before the simulator tiers scale.

Who pays for the bays?

The bay company, never Get Lucky. RMB is modelled at 90% but hasn't committed. Bay investors put up R19.8m over three years, R14.0m of it in 2027. Golfzon wants paying before shipment, so Planet Fitness's Q1 2027 date depends on both. This is our tightest point.

What if the bays sit empty, or Virgin Active says no?

At 70% of planned use the bays cover their debt 0.97× in 2029 and 1.13× at maturity. The bay investors carry the gap (R34.5m instead of R19.8m) and the round still marks at 2.17×. Without Virgin Active, 2.23×.

Isn't this gambling?

No. It's a skill competition with an insured prize: there's no pot, and no golfer's entry funds another golfer's win. We have a written legal opinion for South Africa and clear each market before we sell there.

Straight answers

Investor FAQ

What exactly am I buying?

Now: a convertible note in Get Lucky Golf Club (Pty) Ltd, R1.0m in total, converting into the Q1 2027 round at a R36m cap or a 20% discount, whichever is better for you. Then: ordinary shares, R9.0m for 13.8% at R56m pre-money, from R1.0m. Both are in Get Lucky, not the bay company.

Why two companies?

A bay is an asset that debt can finance; the platform is a growth business that equity should. Split them and your money builds Golfzon and the team, Get Lucky carries no bay debt, and the bays are funded by investors who want an asset-backed investment that pays out. We still earn 6% of everything the bays take, and half their profit once the bay investors are paid back.

Can I invest in the bay company too?

Yes, separately. Bay investors put up R19.8m between 2027 and 2029 and take their capital and 15% a year first; then the profit splits 50/50 with us. Those terms are our proposal. Ask us for the bay company pack.

What does Ernie get?

Equity and a share of profit as Founding Ambassador. No fee, no capital, no guarantees, no liability. His equity is already in the R56m pre-money; the full terms, including how it's treated when we issue shares, are available under NDA.

What if someone wins R1m, or $100,000?

The insurer pays, and it's the best marketing day of our year. 24% of every entry goes to the insurer as premium, on the same swing that creates the risk.
